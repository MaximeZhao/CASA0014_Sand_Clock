# AI Usage

I used ChatGPT and Codex as support during the development of Light Sand. The main uses were debugging, discussing ideas, organising the development process and improving the writing.

## Code and Bug Fixing

I shared compiler errors, serial readings and descriptions of unexpected behaviour with AI. It helped me interpret the problems and suggested changes to parts of the Arduino code. This included checking sensor connections, investigating all-zero accelerometer readings, adjusting shake detection, separating flipping from shaking, and changing touch from holding to a tap-to-toggle interaction. AI also helped draft and revise parts of the code.

## Discussing Ideas

I used the conversation to explore possible interactions and compare them with the components available to me. This helped me move from the original magic-wand idea towards a light hourglass and consider how flipping, shaking, touch and light could work together.

## Summaries and Project Structure

AI helped summarise our development conversations into an iteration timeline. These summaries helped me organise the project framework and GitHub documentation, connecting the initial ideas, problems, changes and test results. AI also helped draft and structure parts of the documentation.

## Writing and Grammar

I used AI to correct grammar, translate mixed Chinese and English passages, and improve the clarity of the writing. I also provided my own writing as a reference so that revisions could keep a more personal, first-person style.

## Design References and Responsibility

AI also supported early enclosure concept images and video/storyboard planning. These were design references rather than photographs or recordings of a working prototype.

My work included selecting the interaction, connecting the components, building the enclosure, testing the device and providing feedback on its behaviour. Physical test results are recorded separately from AI suggestions and software checks. I remain responsible for the final code, documentation, source attribution and reported results.
