# Enclosure and USB routing

## Current construction
The user retained an82×55mm breadboard with the MKR mounted on it, cardboard and transparent side sheets. A triangular-section shell was explored by closing two sides over an existing base. The USB port was initially downward-facing; rotating the entire MKR/breadboard assembly90° in its mounting plane was proposed to allow side access. This rotation was a placement proposal, not proof of a completed modification.

Keep the accelerometer mounting orientation unchanged, secure loose modules and strain-relieve the USB cable. Do not crush jumper heads or use the socket as structural support. Keep an accessible cover for repair.

## Drawings
Concept drawings are in [media/concepts](../media/concepts/). They predate some construction decisions and are not measured cutting templates. Earlier rectangular-shell and later triangular-shell measurements must not be combined.
