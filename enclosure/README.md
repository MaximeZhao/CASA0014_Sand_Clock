# Enclosure Design and Construction

## Materials and Arrangement

The enclosure uses cardboard and transparent sheet panels. The MKR WiFi 1010 remains mounted on an 82 × 55 mm breadboard, with jumper and Grove cables connecting the accelerometer and touch sensor.

During construction, two side panels were brought together over an existing base to explore a triangular cross-section. A sloped support was also considered for the board assembly. The USB cable needed access from the outside, and the acceptance sheet records the fitted cable arrangement as PASS.

These dimensions were confirmed by the project owner on 4 November 2026. The front and back central rectangles use the earlier 6 × 5 cm design dimensions, confirmed as retained in the built model.

## Assembly Requirements

The connected board assembly needs clearance for jumper heads, Grove plugs and natural cable bends. The accelerometer must stay fixed relative to the shell, and the touch surface must remain accessible. USB cable strain should be supported by the shell rather than the board socket.
